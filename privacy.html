Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-21

This application ("the App") is a personal content-management tool. It only accesses the TikTok/Instagram/YouTube account of its own operator, via OAuth, solely to upload and publish video content that the operator created.

The App does not collect, store, or share personal data belonging to any third party. Authentication tokens are stored only to allow the operator to publish their own content and are never shared with any other party.

The App does not use data for advertising, does not sell data, and does not retain content beyond what is required to complete the publishing action.

To request deletion of any stored token or data, contact: dmzolot@gmail.com
